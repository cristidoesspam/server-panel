              Waiting to get in
              
          
                
        
                
        
                
          
                People with access
                
          
                Each device that was let in (with a password, an invite or a yes from you) keeps its own key. Take someone out and their device is locked out at once.
                
          
                  
        
                  
        
                  
          
                  Passwords
                  
          
                  A password is the simplest way in: type it once on each device and it stays signed in for a year. The owner password gives full access. An optional friends password gives only what you tick, for people you trust with the same thing. Remove a device above to lock it out; change a password here and new devices need the new one.

                  Health check
                  
          
                  Looks at the disk, memory, internet, the files the panel needs and each server, and says what is wrong. If something does not work, send the report to whoever helps you.
                  
          
                  
          
                    
        
                    
        
                    
          
                    Alerts
                    
          
                    A message to your phone or to Discord when something happens. For your phone, install the free ntfy app and subscribe to a topic name only you know. For Discord, make a webhook in a channel's settings (Integrations) and paste its address; the panel never shows it again.
                    
          
                    
            
            
            
            
            
                    
            
                    
          
                    
        
                    
        
                    
          
                    Activity
                    
          
                    What people did through the panel, newest first.
                    
          
                      
        
                      
        
                      
          
                      Add a person by name
                      
          
                      Like on Aternos: type their name and tick what they may do. You get a link that fills their name in and lets that one device straight in. If they only type the name, they wait like anyone else and you say yes with one tap. Check the list afterwards to see who came in.
                      
          
                      
            
            
            
            
            
                      
            
            
            
                      
          
                      
          
                      
          
                        
        
                        
        
                        
          
                        Invite someone
                        
          
                        Makes a one-time link that works for one device. They open it, type their name and they are in, with only what you tick.

                                        Move a server here
                                        
          
                                        Bring over a server you made somewhere else, for example on Aternos. The panel reads what is in your download, makes a new server with the same software and Minecraft version (so mods and plugins keep working) and puts the world, mods or plugins and their data, settings, player lists, name and picture into it.
                                        
          
                                          
            
                                        1. On aternos.org open your server. On the Worlds page download the world; on the Files page select everything and download too, if you want the mods, plugins and config. You get .zip files.
                                          2. 
            
                                        2. Choose the .zip below (or a Modrinth modpack, a .mrpack file: its mods are downloaded and checked). The more of the server is in it, the more is carried over. You can add the next zip afterwards with "Put it into a server I already have".
                                          3. 
            
                                        3. Nothing changes until you press the button, and a world you already have is kept as a backup folder.
                                          4. 
          
                                        
          
                                        Aternos has no way to export automatically, and this panel never logs in to your Aternos account: you download the files yourself. Bedrock Edition worlds cannot be used.
